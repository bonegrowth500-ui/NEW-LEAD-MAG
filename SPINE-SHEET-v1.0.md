## 1 · The Spine Sheet

**Archetype.** 🧩 The Framework, primary, with exactly one borrowed 🔍 Diagnostic module.

> "This is a Framework, with one borrowed Diagnostic module — the tally and its named-weakest-gate readout — for a solution-aware and disillusioned Struggler 6–24 months into self-directed work who has seen nothing visibly change and is starting to suspect it's his genetics."

**Validated topic statement.** A Framework (plus one borrowed Diagnostic module) for a solution-aware, disillusioned Struggler 6–24 months into self-directed work who has seen nothing visibly change and is starting to suspect it's his genetics, that helps him locate which of four stress-physiology gates is most likely closed in his own case and know the first thing to change — so he can stop spending effort upstream of a gate that is shut.

*The template's final clause normally reads "so they can ⟨next step⟩." It ends in the reader's own capability because §7 locks the next step to none. That is the statement reporting the architecture, not a gap.*

**Reframe.** Method: Hidden-Variable Reveal (locked).

> **Before:** "I've put a year of real work into this and nothing has changed — and I'm starting to think it's my genetics."
>
> **After:** "Before genetics gets the blame, something upstream has to be true: your body has to be in a state that permits bone formation. Stress is the part of that state this guide maps — it works through cortisol, and cortisol closes four specific gates between the work you do and bone actually being built. Those four you can check yourself. Genetics may still be part of your answer. It just isn't the first thing to look at, and you haven't looked at the four yet."

**Frame** (pp.1–3). "Stress doesn't act on your face. It acts through cortisol — and cortisol closes four specific gates between the work you do and bone actually being built. So before genetics gets the blame, the useful question was never how hard you're working. It's which of the four is shut in your case."

**Capability.** Locked (§3): the reader can locate which gate is most likely closed in their own case, and knows the first thing to change.

**Quick win.** Locked (Q18A): a permissive-state self-rating across the four gates yielding one named weakest gate, tallied from the per-gate mini self-checks, ≤10 minutes, landing in the first third.

## 2 · Three corrections to my Step 1 plan

**2.1 The reframe's best line was false against the ledger.** My plan's reframe A ended "stress load is the variable nobody audits." Refuted 3/3, correctly. The ledger's Permissive Metabolic Foundation (MF Ch.26 Layer 1) has four named members — vitamin D, cortisol, IGF-1, melatonin — and the one it calls unassessed, *with a prevalence figure attached*, is **vitamin D** (47.9% below 50 nmol/L; "Layer 1 is not a formality applied to a rare deficient minority. It is a coin-flip"). Stress load carries no prevalence figure anywhere in either file. The clause was a free parameter introduced because it made the reframe land — TheoreticalLoop Ch.18 Failure Mode 2, committed by me. Cut.

**2.2 The reframe's Before was aimed at the wrong reader.** Candidate A's stock Before ("I need better technique / more effort") is the **Learner's** stuck belief on the Stage 2 belief-first decoder, and is the Framework archetype's own native Before. The spec's **primary** reader holds a different one from that same list: "I'm genetically doomed." Taking A verbatim would have aimed the guide's single belief-move at the on-ramp reader — a Law 1 violation. Re-voiced, and pulled back from a settled verdict to the locked suspicion strength ("starting to think," not "so it must be"), because a settled wrong conclusion is the object of a Misconception Flip, not a Hidden-Variable Reveal.

**2.3 The borrowed Diagnostic module was misattributed.** "Mini self-check closing each spread" is **native Framework form**, verbatim (pipeline l.681, l.674, l.686). Grep: "mini self-check" occurs once in the whole pipeline — inside the Framework tab; zero times in the Diagnostic tab. So the old justification ("borrowing only its self-check imports no method") was true but vacuous — nothing was borrowed there. The genuinely borrowed item is the **tally + named-weakest-gate readout** (l.740–741, l.755), which the Framework supplies nowhere. The count is still exactly one.

**Consequence, and it is why the misattribution mattered.** The Diagnostic attaches a mandatory control to *that* instrument — "this audit can point; it can't diagnose" (l.759) — listed separately from the p3 not-a-diagnosis line (l.746). My plan imported only the p3-class line and left the page where the final named verdict lands with no control at all. Both now ship.

**Also restored:** the Framework's per-spread form is **five** slots (l.674) — what it is → why it matters → how to check yours → **common misreads** → what it can't do. "Misread" appeared nowhere in my plan. Restored as one line per gate spread (~25 words, no page cost). It is the archetype's own correction device and the native remedy for this build's largest named risk: lever collapse into "sleep well and stress less."

## 3 · The structural fix that makes the capability deliverable

A refuting lens showed all four gates were composed purely as cellular events, while each gate's own limits line says that gate's state is unknowable from the inside — so "locate which gate is most likely closed in your own case" was **undeliverable as specified**.

Repair: **each gate carries its own reader-visible determinant**, written from that gate's locked mechanism and never from its intervention. That is the Framework form's third slot ("how to check yours"), which my plan had collapsed into the self-check.

**Quick-win placement — impossibility proved, not asserted.** Front matter is ≥3pp (cover; the "if you're new" box; the confidence panel, which §4.2 requires before any mechanism). Four gate treatments at even one page each put the earliest completed tally at p8, needing ≥24pp; at Q11C's two-page spreads it lands at p12 and needs ≥36pp. Q6A locks 10–14. So "tallied from the per-gate mini self-checks" + "first third" + the length band are **jointly unsatisfiable by a single artefact**.

Resolution: **one instrument, read twice.** Each rating row *is* that gate's mini self-check, verbatim — same questions, same verdict words. First pass at p4 is a partial reading of the locked instrument; the spread check re-administers the same rows plus one item the reader could not have assessed earlier; p13 tallies the four completed checks. Locked clause satisfied in substance at p4 and in full at p13, with one instrument rather than two. My plan's version put a *different* instrument in the first third and reported it as the locked quick win unchanged — that was the defect.

**Also:** three criteria per row, not one. Four gates × one criterion over three verdict words cannot rank anything — ties at the worst level are near-certain for this reader.

**Diagram instruction derived (not design taste).** Draw the cascade as a **fan from one cortisol node to four points, never four boxes in series.** MF l.515 enumerates differentiation suppression, fate shift, apoptosis, RANKL activation and Wnt inhibition as *parallel* actions of one agent. Four boxes in series would be a visual claim the ledger does not support.

## 5 · The four gates — RECOMMENDED (Stage 7 owns)

Recast as reader-facing questions, each with a determinant and a limits line.

| Gate | The question it answers | Mechanism | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Fate** | Was the cell ever made? | Glucocorticoids shift mesenchymal fate from osteoblastogenesis toward adipogenesis; C/EBPα promoter methylation reduced, persisting through 21 days of osteogenic induction | Established / **in vitro** for persistence / inferred for transfer to chronic human stress — **three tiers in one spread** |
| **2 · Effectors** | Is the cell that exists alive and finishing its job? | Suppressed osteoblast differentiation; increased osteoblast and osteocyte apoptosis; GIO as the clinical consequence | Established; mouse for the craniofacial arm |

**Mineralization is not a gate.** It isn't cortisol-mediated, so it would break the locked cascade frame. It becomes the **named excluded ceiling on p14**, with its number.

Importing them would have been borrowed authority. This is a firewall Step 2 must hold at ledger level, because that material will pull on any writer who needs Gate 2 to feel consequential.

## 6 · The four interventions — RECOMMENDED (Stage 7 owns)

One per gate, four distinct objects, four distinct actors.

| Gate | Lever | Object acted on |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Fate | **Reduce total load** — write the standing-stressor inventory (anything running >1 month, with start dates), then subtract one thing | The standing load and its calendar · reader |
| 2 · Effectors | **Feed the survival signal** — don't run a chronic deficit while you're waiting for something to build; adequate protein and total intake | What you eat · reader |
| 3 · Signal | **Know your glucocorticoid exposure** — list every corticosteroid you take: inhaled, intranasal, topical, oral, injected, with durations | A prescribed drug · reader + prescriber |
| 4 · Window | **Protect the eight hours** — one schedule all week, seven to eight hours, and the overnight question answered rather than assumed | The clock and the overnight route · reader + clinician |

**Two departures from my plan, both on sourced grounds.**

*Gate 2 is IGF-1 availability, not vitamin D.* Gate 2 is about effector **survival**, and the ledger names cellular survival as an IGF-1 function. Vitamin D governs mineralization, which is no longer a gate.

*Gate 3 is exogenous steroids, not breathing.* Gate 3's mechanism is literally glucocorticoid action on Wnt/RANKL/PTH, so the only lever that passes Operator 32D (mechanism-type matching) is glucocorticoid input the reader controls. Breathing does not type-match this gate; the airway lever moves to Gate 4 where it belongs. **Priced honestly:** this makes Gate 3 inert for the majority who take no steroids. That is the cost of type-matching instead of qualifying around it.
