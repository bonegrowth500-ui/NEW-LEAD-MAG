## 1. Subject and scope

**Topic.** GABA, stress, and how they affect facial development — delivered as a mechanism model plus **3–4 actionable interventions** carried by strong mechanistic inference.

**What the guide is.** A teaching instrument. It explains the cascade from stress physiology to bone-formation permissiveness, names the gates in that cascade, and gives the reader interventions tied to each gate. It makes no outcome claim, routes nowhere, and carries no positioning block.

**What the guide is not.** Not a protocol. Not a diagnosis. Not a results promise. Not a funnel entry — there is no CTA anywhere in it (§7).

## 4. Claim architecture

**Claim ceiling (Q7B):** process claims + **tiered educational claims on mechanism**.

**May say** (examples):
- "Glucocorticoid exposure suppresses osteoblast differentiation and shifts progenitor allocation toward fat." — established, marrow stromal cells
- "Understanding which gate is closed tells you what's worth doing first."
- "GABAergic tone plausibly influences HPA restraint and slow-wave sleep architecture."

**Never say:**
- Any claim that GABA affects facial bone
- Any claim that any intervention produces facial change
- Any dose stated as a prescription
- Any timeline to result
- Anything universal, guaranteed, medical, or replacing professional care
- Any fear clock ("your bones get less malleable every month") — forbidden by Law 7 and by Q30A

### 4.1 The inference chain, tiered

GABA is an **[EXTENSION]** relative to the ledger. Cortisol→bone is well-supported in MF; GABA appears nowhere in it. Each link is tiered separately and the whole chain is marked as inference, prominently, on p1 (Q14C):

| Link | Tier | Ledger |
|---|---|---|
| Chronic stress → sustained cortisol elevation | Established | MF Ch.6, Ch.7 |
| Cortisol → suppressed bone formation (multiple mechanisms) | Established | MF Ch.6, Ch.11, Ch.23 |
| Sleep quality → GH pulsatility / melatonin peak / cortisol nadir convergence | Established | MF Ch.6, Ch.7, Ch.25 |
| GABAergic tone → HPA axis restraint | Plausible | [EXTENSION] — outside ledger |
| GABAergic tone → slow-wave sleep architecture | Plausible | [EXTENSION] — outside ledger |
| Oral GABA → central GABAergic tone | **Contested** (BBB penetration) | [EXTENSION] |
| GABA → craniofacial bone, directly | **Unknown / not studied** — said out loud | — |

### 4.2 Tier signalling surfaces (Q51A)

Compressed by design. Tier signalling lives in exactly two places:
1. The **p1 confidence panel** — minimal (Q24, "more minimal"), one line per chain link, placed before any mechanism
2. **Inline tier words in prose**, only where the claim is weakest

No tier callout style. No per-gate tier badge. No header tier marks. Honest-qualifier density is **minimal** (Q46) — qualifiers appear only where the tier strictly requires them; prose is otherwise declarative.

> **Consequence, accepted:** claim discipline has fewer surfaces than the pipeline default. The p1 panel must be complete even while minimal, or the chain is under-labelled.

### 4.3 Citation handling (Q49 — OPEN)

Either: **(A)** named mechanism + tier word, no formal citations; or **(B)** mechanism + tier + a light in-line source mention ("shown in marrow stromal cells"). Both are consistent with proof inventory = competence + process only (Q8A). Default if unresolved: **B** — it is the stronger competence signal at no extra claim risk.

### 4.4 Scope boundary (Q29B)

**One consolidated 🚩 page only.** No per-gate 🚩 boxes, no inline 🚩 lines. Content:
- Sleep-disordered breathing signs (loud snoring, gasping, unrefreshing sleep)
- Medication interactions, especially anything acting on GABA-A, sedatives, SSRIs
- Under-18s
- Diagnosed anxiety or depression under treatment
- Jaw pain, clicking, locking; bite changes; anything post-surgical

### 4.5 The durable epigenetic mark (Q30A)

The C/EBPα adipogenic bias that outlasts the stressor (dexamethasone reduces promoter methylation; persists after 21 days of osteogenic induction — MF Ch.6, Ch.11) is stated **plainly as mechanism, framed forward**: *this is why total load matters more than any single moment.* No timeline. No urgency language. Not led with (Q30A, not C).

## 5. The Four Gates

Candidate set (Q21 — OPEN, OPEN-03). Working set = Q21A:

| Gate | Mechanism | Ledger |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Fate** | Glucocorticoids shift mesenchymal progenitor allocation from osteoblastogenesis toward adipogenesis; the C/EBPα methylation change is durable and outlasts the exposure | MF Ch.6, Ch.11 |
| **2 · Effectors** | Glucocorticoids suppress osteoblast differentiation and increase osteoblast and osteocyte apoptosis. Osteocyte loss is structurally irreversible — empty lacunae are not refilled | MF Ch.6, Ch.23; TL Ch.6 |
| **3 · Signal** | Glucocorticoids inhibit Wnt through multiple routes and activate RANKL — brake on and accelerator on simultaneously. They also blunt the anabolic PTH response | MF Ch.6; TL Ch.4 |
| **4 · Window** | GH pulsatility in slow-wave sleep, melatonin peak and cortisol nadir **coincide only during quality sleep** and cannot be reproduced with the same temporal coordination. Sleep-disordered breathing degrades the mechanical, hormonal and possibly epigenetic substrate in the same eight hours | MF Ch.6, Ch.7, Ch.25 |

**Alternate / candidate fifth (Q21B/C):** mineralization substrate — vitamin D insufficiency (47.9% of the global population below 50 nmol/L) caps the terminal step; osteoid that cannot mineralize is not bone. MF Ch.6, Ch.26 Layer 1, Ch.31 Ceiling 4. Resolve at OPEN-03.

## 6. Interventions

**Count:** 3–4. **Placement:** distributed inside each gate spread, not collected into a back section (Q31B).
**Class scope (Q19C):** behavioural/physiological **plus supplement protocols included as interventions.**

**Agent set (Q25 — OPEN, flexible, no cortisol-axis focus).** Reading: the cascade stays cortisol-mediated because that is the mechanism, but the *agent set* avoids cortisol-axis adaptogens (no ashwagandha, no phosphatidylserine). GABAergic / sleep-axis only. Candidate pool: glycine, magnesium, L-theanine, apigenin, taurine — plus **GABA itself discussed honestly**, including the contested BBB-penetration question.

**Dose specificity (Q26 — OPEN):** either **(A)** named compound + the range used in human trials, with "this is what was studied, not a prescription" plus interaction and scope routing; or **(B)** compound only, no numbers. Default if unresolved: **A** — it is more useful and the framing carries the risk, provided the 🚩 page is in place.

**Per-agent presentation (Q27D):** grouped into **"reasonable to try / thin / don't bother."** No agent-by-agent tier table, no "who should not" column (that load moves to the consolidated 🚩 page).

**Candidate intervention set (Q28 — OPEN, OPEN-05):**
1. Protect the overnight convergence window — continuity, timing regularity, nasal breathing
2. Reduce total stress **load**, not momentary stress — justified by the durable mark (§4.5)
3. GABAergic / sleep-axis support — behavioural (breathing cadence, light timing) + supplemental (§6 agent set)
4. Mineralization and substrate competence — vitamin D status **verified, not assumed** (MF Operator 32E)

## 7. No-CTA architecture (Q20A + Q34D + Q36D + Q39C)

Four decisions compound here and the result is deliberate:

- **No destination, no link, no ask** anywhere in the guide (Q20A)
- **No dedicated "where self-assessment runs out" demonstration page.** The limit is folded into the limits page without a worked ambiguity (Q34D)
- **Close is recap only.** Limits are handled earlier; the guide simply ends (Q36D)
- **No "About this channel" block.** Omitted entirely (Q39C)

> **Design consequence, accepted and flagged.** Every structural trust device the pipeline supplies has been removed. Desire for credible judgment must form **organically**, from the quality of the mechanism treatment and the honesty of the limits page alone. There is no device carrying it. This raises the execution bar on §4, §5 and the limits page specifically — they are now the only things doing that work.

## 12. Standing tensions

Recorded so they are not rediscovered mid-build.

1. **No CTA vs. Law 1 / "Routes."** Resolved by §7: no ask, desire formed organically. The pipeline's routing requirement is knowingly waived.
2. **GABA as title-level hero vs. Law 5 proportionality.** Resolved by OPEN-06: the title promises an **explanation**, not an effect. An explanation-promise stands without a caveat.
3. **GABA-forward billing vs. GABA being an [EXTENSION].** Resolved by §4.1 and the p1 confidence panel: the chain is labelled, the unknown link is named out loud.
4. **Supplement protocols as interventions** is the spec's highest claim-risk choice. Guardrails: dose framed as "what was studied, not a prescription" (OPEN-09), grouped tiers rather than per-agent claims (Q27D), consolidated 🚩 page (§4.4), no supplement imagery is **not** enforced (Q57C chose defaults as written, which does not include it).
5. **Compressed tier surfaces** (§4.2). The p1 panel is load-bearing. If it is thin, the guide is under-labelled.
6. **Minimal structural trust devices** (§7). Execution quality in §4/§5 and the limits page is the only thing carrying conversion intent.
